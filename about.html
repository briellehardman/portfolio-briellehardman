A little context

People, stories, and useful ideas.

I'm Brielle Hardman, a senior at Texas Christian University studying Communication Studies with a minor in Digital Culture and Data Analytics.

Background

From California to Texas

I'm originally from California and chose TCU because I wanted to experience college in a new state and become more independent. Moving to Texas gave me the opportunity to meet new people, try new things, and experience a different environment from where I grew up.

My studies bring together communication, digital culture, and data analytics. I enjoy thinking about how people encounter information and what makes a message or experience feel clear, useful, and human.

Looking ahead

What I want to keep learning

I'm interested in how apps are designed and built. I want to keep growing more confident with coding and learn how to turn an idea into something people can actually use. The web development skills I've built in this course are a foundation I plan to keep developing after graduation.

Interests

Digital experiences, grounded in real life

I'm drawn to the everyday details that shape how people connect with places, food, and one another. That perspective shows up in my work, from organizing local favorites to making restaurant and recipe discovery easier to navigate.

Experience
